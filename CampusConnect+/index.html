The Problem

Schools get flooded with identical questions: "When are the exams?", "Is tomorrow a holiday?", "Can I see the fee receipt?" Processing these manually frustrates parents and drains administrative staff hours.

The AI Solution

CampusConnect+ automates the first line of communication. Available 24/7, it retrieves dynamic data instantly, allowing parents to get answers, pay fees, and download documents through a friendly chat interface.

What It Can Do

📊

Attendance

Instantly check daily attendance, request term reports, or submit absence notifications.

📝

Exams & Results

Fetch real-time exam schedules, access latest grade results, and download secure admit cards.

💳

Fee Management

Review due dates, generate past receipts, and link out to secure payment gateways.
